# NMM 100k–150k benchmark — 15 October 2026

All 20 manifest models were processed: 19 completed and qap15 reached the 60-second solve limit on both solvers. All 95 timed completed-model outcome pairs matched exactly on status, iterations, and objective bits. All 19 completed models reported OPTIMAL. No solver code was changed.

Sum of completed-model medians: **58.878101 s native / 56.043680 s Rust**, **0.951860x** Rust/native (4.81% less time). Median model ratio: 0.964177x. Sqrt(nnz)-weighted geometric ratio: 0.973660x. Rust was faster on 12 of the 19 completed models. These totals exclude qap15.

## Harness correction and limited case

The initial campaign stopped at qap15: the harness correctly detected the wall limit, but then incorrectly asserted the completed Netlib fixture status/iterations against its partial state. The fixture assertion now applies only to nonlimited runs, preserving all completed-case checks. The full campaign was restarted after this one-line harness repair; the raw report below comes solely from that restart. The solver binaries were unchanged. The successful full campaign exercises both branches of the repaired assertion.

qap15 has one timed pair (the harness stops a limited case after that pair). Both solvers reported PRIMAL_FEASIBLE at approximately 60 seconds; native reached 42,262 iterations and Rust 45,204. Objectives differ because wall-clock limits stop at different points. This is partial progress, not a completed-result agreement or proof of convergence. No qap15 speed ratio is claimed.
