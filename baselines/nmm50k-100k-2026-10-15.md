# NMM 50k–100k benchmark — 15 October 2026

Current Rust compared with pinned native GLOP using the default public solver pipeline. All 25 manifest models completed; all 125 timed outcome pairs matched exactly on status, iterations, and objective bits. No wall limits were reached. No solver code was changed.

The sum of per-model medians was **25.200973 s native / 23.955552 s Rust**, or **0.950580x** Rust/native (4.94% less time). The median model ratio was 0.961235x and the sqrt(nnz)-weighted geometric ratio was 0.949585x. Rust was faster on 23 of 25 models.

## Conditions and scope

Benchmark mode was verified before measurement and again after the full run: recorded cloud/photo/search workers were stopped, indexing was disabled on all three recorded stores, and caffeinate was active. The preparation script dry run found no additional workers/stores to suspend. Mode was left on. Desktop activity was not otherwise isolated; small timing differences remain subject to noise.
